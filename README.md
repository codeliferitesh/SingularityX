# SingularityX

> **Building things. Breaking limits.**  
> An independent developer and hardware hackathon team from India.

---

## ⚡ Overview

**SingularityX** is the official portfolio and team website for five friends who build software, experiment with hardware, and spend their time turning ideas into working prototypes.

Designed with an authentic developer aesthetic: editorial typography, generous whitespace, thin borders, understated accents, and zero corporate fluff.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Three Visual Modes (Theme Switcher)**:
  - **Dark Mode**: Near-black charcoal background, soft white typography, restrained contrast.
  - **Light Mode**: Warm off-white editorial paper feel with crisp charcoal text.
  - **Retro Mode**: Late 90s / early 2000s computer lab aesthetic with full monospace typography, CRT scanline overlay, and a live terminal status bar.
  - Fully persistent via `localStorage` and respects system `prefers-color-scheme`.
- **Live India Standard Time (IST) Clock**:
  - Live time clock synchronized to the `Asia/Kolkata` (UTC+05:30) timezone in the navigation header, retro status bar, and footer dispatch.
- **Handcrafted Orbital Visual**:
  - Pure SVG coordinate diagram in the hero section representing all 5 members orbiting the central singularity.
- **Disciplines & What We Build**:
  - `01 — Software`: Web applications and digital tools.
  - `02 — Hardware`: Embedded systems, circuits, and microcontrollers.
  - `03 — Experiments`: Hackathon prototypes and exploratory builds.
- **Interactive Contact & Dispatch**:
  - Direct email dispatch with one-click clipboard copy.
- **Lightweight & Fast**:
  - Built with Vite and React with zero external UI/component library bloat. Zero layout shift.
